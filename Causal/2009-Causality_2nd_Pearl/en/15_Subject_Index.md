# Subject Index

> **Note** : This index is a work in progress. Some entries may be incomplete or missing.

## A

- **Abstract algebra** : Study of algebraic structures such as groups, rings, and fields.
- **Algorithm** : A finite sequence of well-defined instructions for solving a problem.
- **Analytic geometry** : Study of geometry using a coordinate system and algebraic principles.

## B

- **Basis (linear algebra)** : A set of linearly independent vectors that span a vector space.
- **Bifurcation theory** : Study of qualitative changes in the behavior of dynamical systems.
- **Boolean algebra** : Algebraic structure capturing the essence of logical operations.

## C

- **Calculus** : Branch of mathematics focusing on limits, functions, derivatives, integrals, and infinite series.
- **Category theory** : Abstract framework for studying mathematical structures and their relationships.
- **Combinatorics** : Study of finite or discrete structures and counting problems.
- **Complex analysis** : Study of functions of complex variables.
- **Convex optimization** : Optimization of convex functions over convex sets.

## D

- **Differential equation** : Equation relating a function with its derivatives.
- **Differential geometry** : Study of geometry using calculus and linear algebra.
- **Discrete mathematics** : Study of mathematical structures that are fundamentally discrete.

## E

- **Eigenvalue problem** : Finding scalars $\lambda$ and vectors $v$ such that $A v = \lambda v$ .
- **Ergodic theory** : Study of statistical properties of dynamical systems.
- **Euclidean geometry** : Geometry based on Euclid's axioms and postulates.

## F

- **Field theory** : Study of algebraic structures called fields.
- **Fourier analysis** : Decomposition of functions into sinusoidal components.
- **Functional analysis** : Study of vector spaces with a limit-related structure (e.g., Banach spaces, Hilbert spaces).

## G

- **Game theory** : Study of strategic decision-making among rational agents.
- **Graph theory** : Study of graphs as mathematical structures modeling pairwise relations.
- **Group theory** : Study of algebraic groups and their properties.

## H

- **Homological algebra** : Study of homology in algebraic contexts.
- **Hyperbolic geometry** : Non-Euclidean geometry with constant negative curvature.

## I

- **Information theory** : Study of quantification, storage, and communication of information.
- **Integral equation** : Equation where an unknown function appears under an integral sign.
- **Invariant theory** : Study of invariants under group actions.

## K

- **K-theory** : Study of vector bundles and topological invariants.
- **Knot theory** : Study of mathematical knots and their properties.

## L

- **Lie algebra** : Algebraic structure closely related to Lie groups.
- **Lie group** : Group that is also a differentiable manifold.
- **Linear algebra** : Study of vector spaces and linear transformations.
- **Logic** : Study of valid reasoning and formal systems.

## M

- **Manifold theory** : Study of topological spaces locally resembling Euclidean space.
- **Mathematical physics** : Application of mathematics to physical problems.
- **Measure theory** : Study of measures and measurable functions.
- **Metric geometry** : Study of geometry based on distance functions.
- **Modular forms** : Complex analytic functions with certain transformation properties.

## N

- **Number theory** : Study of integers and integer-valued functions.
- **Numerical analysis** : Study of algorithms for continuous mathematics.

## O

- **Operator theory** : Study of linear operators on function spaces.
- **Optimization** : Selection of a best element from a set of alternatives.
- **Ordinary differential equation (ODE)** : Differential equation with one independent variable.

## P

- **Partial differential equation (PDE)** : Differential equation with multiple independent variables.
- **Probability theory** : Study of random phenomena and uncertainty.
- **Pseudocode** : Informal description of an algorithm's steps, often formatted as an unordered list.

> **Example pseudocode** :
>
> - Input: matrix $A$ of size $m \times n$
> - For $i = 1$ to $m$ :
>   - For $j = 1$ to $n$ :
>     - Compute $A_{ij} = A_{ij} + 1$
> - Output: modified matrix $A$

## Q

- **Quadratic form** : Homogeneous polynomial of degree two.
- **Quantum calculus** : Calculus without limits, using $q$ -differences.

## R

- **Real analysis** : Study of real numbers and real-valued functions.
- **Representation theory** : Study of abstract algebraic structures via linear transformations.
- **Riemannian geometry** : Study of smooth manifolds with Riemannian metrics.

## S

- **Set theory** : Study of sets, which are collections of objects.
- **Statistical inference** : Drawing conclusions from data subject to random variation.
- **Stochastic processes** : Collection of random variables indexed by time or space.
- **Symplectic geometry** : Study of symplectic manifolds and Hamiltonian mechanics.

## T

- **Tensor analysis** : Study of tensors and their transformations.
- **Topology** : Study of properties preserved under continuous deformations.
- **Trigonometry** : Study of angles and trigonometric functions.

## U

- **Universal algebra** : Study of algebraic structures in a general setting.

## V

- **Variational calculus** : Study of functionals and their extremal points.
- **Vector calculus** : Study of vector fields and differential operators.

## W

- **Wavelet theory** : Decomposition of functions into wavelets.
- **Weak convergence** : Convergence in the sense of distributions or measures.

## Z

- **Zeta function** : Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1$ .
- **Zorn's lemma** : Axiom of choice equivalent used in set theory.
